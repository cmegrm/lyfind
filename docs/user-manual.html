1. What LyFind is

LyFind is the CSA lost-and-found website. It records items that reach the office, compares lost reports with those items, invites a likely owner, and records the checks before the item leaves the desk.

The five steps you will see on cards:

StepMeaning
1 ReportSomeone reported a lost item, or a found item is waiting to be turned in.
2 MatchCSA reviews a suggested pair and may invite the owner.
3 VerifyThe owner answers private questions. CSA then checks ID at the office.
4 Hand overPhotos are on file. Staff give the item to the owner.
5 DoneThe report is closed. Nobody else can claim that item.
The website does not replace the desk. A match score is a hint. A valid ID at the CSA office is still required. LyFind never hands an item over by itself.

2. How to open the website

  1. On the computer that has this project, open Terminal.
  2. Go to the LyFind folder, then run python3 main.py.
  3. Keep that window open. In a browser, visit http://127.0.0.1:8765.
  4. Use Sign in or Sign up. Help on the site explains each role.

Optional, once: python3 setup_ai.py installs local photo matching (about 1.5 GB, needs internet once). Without it the site still works; Matches will say AI is off and use typed details only.

If port 8765 is busy, run python3 main.py --port 8766 and open that address instead.

4. Students

If you lost something

  1. Sign in with your LPU email and password. The first time, open the account with the emailed code, then choose that password.
  2. Choose Report lost.
  3. Say what it is, the category, color, brand if you know it, where, and when.
  4. Add identifying marks (keychain, scratch, initials). These help more than a generic description.
  5. Add a photo of your item if you have one — not a similar photo from the internet. Fill the frame, even light, include a mark only you would know.
  6. Submit. Follow the item on Home and in Updates.

If CSA invites you

  1. Open My claims (or the invitation in Updates).
  2. Choose Claim item. Describe something only the owner would know. Do not type a password.
  3. Tick the box and Send. Wait for CSA to read the answers.
  4. When asked, go to the CSA office with a valid ID.
  5. Staff will check the item with you, take photos (you with the item, your ID, your signature), then hand it over.

If you found something

Choose Report found, then bring the physical item to the CSA office. It cannot be claimed until CSA receives it and photographs it.

5. Professors

Sign in is the same as students: LPU email and password, after a one-time code when the account is opened.

  • Report lost the same way a student does.
  • Report found if you picked something up. Set when you will bring it in. CSA still has to receive it at the office before anyone can claim it.
  • When invited, answer the ownership questions and bring ID to CSA.

6. Guests

  1. Sign up, confirm your email with the code, then sign in with email and password.
  2. You can Report lost only. CSA checks each guest report before matching starts. That reduces pranks.
  3. If you found an item, take it straight to the CSA office. Do not file an online found report as a guest.
  4. If CSA invites you, answer the questions and bring a valid ID to pick it up.
Guest reports wait Staff see them under To do → Guest reports to check. Matching does not run until a staff member marks the report as checked (or hides it if it looks fake).

7. CSA staff and admin — daily work

Sign in with the staff email and password the office gave you. Your home page is To do.

7.1 Start at To do

The four cards are buttons. Use them every shift:

CardWhat to do
WaitingClaims that need an online check, an office check, photos, or handover.
MatchesSuggested pairs to Approve or Reject. Strongest first.
In the officeItems already received. Open Found items.
Done this weekHandovers already recorded.

Also on To do: guest reports that still need a human check, and an AI is on or AI is off card so you know whether photos were compared by the local models.

7.2 Add a found item (receive)

Only after the object is physically on the desk.

  1. Press Add found item (or open a found report that is awaiting turn-in and receive it from there).
  2. Confirm category, description, place, and date found. Date cannot be in the future. Found-before-lost pairs are later hidden by matching.
  3. Set color and brand. Wrong color or brand will cap a match score.
  4. Keep-for days defaults to 30. After that, unclaimed items can be marked Not claimed under Admin → Clean up.
  5. Private details (required): something only the owner would know — initials inside, a receipt, a charm. Owners never see this text. You will compare it with their answers.
  6. Photo of the item (required): photograph the real object in front of you. JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 5 MB. Fill the frame. Two different bags that only “look similar” should not match.
  7. Save. The item appears under Found items, and matching runs right away.

7.3 Lost reports and guest checks

  • Open Lost reports to see every owner report and its step.
  • For a guest report: read it. If it looks real, Mark as checked. Matching can then run. If it looks like a prank, Hide report (the record stays; you can restore it). Give a reason. Hiding is logged.
  • Do not read private found-item details aloud to a claimant. Compare silently.

7.4 Found items

Tabs: In the office (claimable inventory) and On the way (found reports not yet received). Filter by status: available, being checked, handed back, not claimed. Open an item to see the staff-only photo and private details.

8. AI matching — how to use and check it

This is the part staff use to decide whether the owner’s photo is the same physical item that was surrendered to CSA.

8.1 What the AI actually does

  • It runs on this computer with DINOv2 and CLIP. Photos never leave the server.
  • DINOv2 compares the owner’s photo with the office photo (same physical item vs a different object of the same type).
  • CLIP compares typed words with a photo when that helps (for example, no owner photo).
  • If both photos exist and they do not look like the same object, matching words cannot save the pair. It stays hidden.
  • If models are missing, Matches shows AI is off and uses typed details only. Typed details alone never reach Strong.

8.2 How to review a match

  1. Open Matches. The badge is 1 waiting when something needs you.
  2. Use tabs: To review (your job), Invited (you already approved; waiting for the owner), All.
  3. Look at the two pictures: Owner’s photo vs Item in the office. Ask: is this the same bag/wallet that CSA received, or only the same kind of object?
  4. Open Why this match?. Read every bar: photo looks alike, words fit the photo, description words, category, color, brand, place, date.
  5. Read private details on the found item (View item) and the owner’s identifying marks. Do they agree?
  6. If it is the same item: Approve, tick “I checked the details.” The owner is invited to answer questions. They still must show ID later.
  7. If it is not the same item: Reject. The owner is not told. The item stays available for other reports.
LabelScoreWhat it means for you
Strong80 and upPhotos (or a full set of signals) look like the same item. Still check with your eyes before you invite.
Possible60 to 79Overlap in details, but photos did not prove it. Be extra careful.
Not shownBelow 60LyFind hides the pair. You should not treat it as a match.
How to check the photos yourself Same item: same shape, same badge or zipper, same marks, even if one photo is a tighter crop. Different item: another backpack of the same color is not a match. If you are unsure, Reject and wait for a better report, or ask the person more questions at the desk. Never Approve only because the percentage is 100%.

8.3 Find matches

Matching also runs when you save a lost report or receive an item. Press Find matches after a batch of new reports, or if AI was off and you later turned it on. A rerun updates open suggestions. Matches you already approved or rejected are kept.

8.4 What owners never see

Scores, found-item photos, private details, and the exact found place stay hidden until you approve a match. After invitation they still do not see the private notes; they only answer questions.

9. Claims: answers, office check, photos, handover

Open Claims (or Waiting on To do). Work in this order. The site will refuse if you skip a step.

9.1 Online check (answers)

  1. Open the claim. Read the owner’s answers.
  2. Open the item and compare with Private details. Do not coach the claimant.
  3. If they match: Approve. Optional note. Tick “I checked this myself.” The owner is told to bring ID.
  4. If they do not match: Reject with a reason the owner will see. The item goes back to the shelf. That person has three tries on the same item; after that they are blocked from claiming that item only.

9.2 Office check (the person is here)

  1. The owner must be at the desk. Press Start check.
  2. Record what you told them (default: to check ownership and record the handover).
  3. Tick that they are here and agree to the photos.
  4. Check the ID against the person. Compare answers again if needed.
  5. Approve the office check, or Reject with a reason.

9.3 Evidence photos, then Hand over

  1. Add three photos: owner with the item, ID photo, signature. JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 5 MB each.
  2. Tick that you checked the person, the photos, and both earlier checks. Approve.
  3. Press Hand over. Tick that the owner has the item now. This cannot be undone.
  4. The claim and lost report close. Activity records who handed it over and when.
Always required Valid ID at the office. No ID, no handover — even if the match was Strong and the answers were perfect.

11. Privacy, ID, and what never to skip

  • Other students and guests never see someone else’s reports, answers, or photos.
  • Found-item photos and private details are staff-only until the workflow allows a claim.
  • Hide is not delete. Prank reports stay in the database and the log.
  • One active claim per item. Released items cannot be claimed again.
  • Most items are kept 30 days unless you set another keep time when receiving.
